WAVEZ TOOLS =========== Quality of life tools for wavez.fm. I've spent a lot of nights in the rooms and the same small annoyances kept turning up. Chat I couldn't collapse. Songs I wanted on Spotify. Images that wouldn't load in my region. So I fixed them, one script at a time. Nothing official. INSTALL ------- 1. Get Tampermonkey (or any userscript manager). 2. Click a script below. They self-update after that. SCRIPTS ------- translate.user.js ........ translate chat, inline, customisable open-in-spotify.user.js .. find the currently playing song on spotify chat-toggle.user.js ...... collapsible rail imgur-fix.user.js ........ unbreak imgur auto-woot.user.js ........ woot everything automatically auto-grab.user.js ........ automatically grab what you woot Source: github.com/fluteds/wavez Not affiliated with wavez.fm.
