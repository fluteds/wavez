Wavez Tools.

Quality of life tools for wavez.fm

I spend a lot of time on wavez.fm and kept bumping into the same small annoyances, chat I couldn't collapse, songs I wanted on Spotify, images that wouldn't load in my region. So I "fixed" them, one script at a time. Nothing official, just tweaks that make my nights in the rooms nicer. Open source, so fork it and make it yours.

Needs Tampermonkey or your userscript manager of choice.

Translate Translate chat and system messages inline. Open in Spotify Searches the currently playing song on Spotify. Chat Toggle Collapsible chat rail with an unread dot. Imgur Fix Reroute region-blocked Imgur images through Rimgo.
